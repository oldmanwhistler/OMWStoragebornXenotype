## Equipment

There is no clothing or armour supported for the body types because I started this off very much as reimagining of the Skyrim Kallax Storageborn mod.

I was originally not going to allow any equipment because of these are "objects", but that felt silly when I add robots and living creatures, so I decided some wouldn't be able to equip weapons and would instead have bonuses to melee and other abilities.

They are xenolocked and can't get further xenogenes because they aren't set up with proper bodies and heads, so adding further cosmetic genes won't work properly.

## Skills

The original idea was that the xenotype would have no skills so they would only be for transporting, cleaning, etc. But that made it impossible to use them in small colonies.

## Genes

I tried to balance the gene metabolism but it's an imperfect system. Looking at the raw genes they would have the max hunger because the Big & Small combination of no armour and no equipment is such a huge debuff.

The genes I added to thematically account for being "objects" also drive the metabolism further in the wrong direction.

In the end, I decided to say fuck it and add genes for what thematically made sense without caring too much about metabolism and then just set an offset based on the race. I did try to use aptitude genes to balance the sub-types against each other.

Psycasting genes were done thematically as well vs trying to achieve any game balance.

## Balance

The lack of armour and utility will make them overpowered for neolithic eras but then more balanced at higher tech eras.

I had looked at the B&S "android fuel" diet for the "robot" storageborn but it was too hard to play with in early colonies.
